AboutHeader Image Welcome to NOMISMATA database, geo and chrono reference of Byzantine Coin Finds (hoards, single finds and foundation deposits) Current version is 1.0 (beta version) - January 2023 You can navigate via 4 header menus: Byzantine Coin Find- Mints- Coins- Sources You have also a Filter menu from where you pick your filter options When you select one or more filters you access the Menu bar (middle center screen) from where you can List, Map, Network, Chart the results of your queries By clicking on any object (dot, list, bar, etc.) you get access to the master data behind Middle right screen there is a legend, from where you can switch on/off what you see on the map Bottom screen there is the time slider you can use to diachronically see your data Any kind of feedback (especially bugs, imperfections, missing infos, etc.) is more than welcome. Pls address your feedback to: Giorgio Donato - Independent Researcher at University of Trieste (Italy) - Department of Humanities - GIORGIO.DONATO@studenti.units.it or giorgio.donato22@gmail.com
